Sound familiar?

Most of us know we should move. The hard part is fitting it into real life.

An hour-long workout sounds great in theory. On a real day, it gets pushed aside.

  • 1
    Days are full - and energy runs out Between work, family, and screens, a long workout feels like one more task you won’t get to.
  • 2
    No gym nearby, and no gear at home When training depends on a place or equipment, it’s easy to quit the moment conditions aren’t perfect.
  • 3
    Start strong - disappear after a week When the bar is too high, every miss feels like failure. Then you stop altogether.
The idea

Exercise snacks are short, focused bursts of activity that fit easily into your day.

Instead of waiting for a long workout, you sprinkle short movement breaks throughout the day - usually 1-5 minutes, from light to high intensity. Almost anywhere, often with no equipment.
